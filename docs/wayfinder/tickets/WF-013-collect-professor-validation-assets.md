# Collect academic-supervisor validation assets and constraints

## Question

Obtain the academic supervisor's representative PowerPoint template, a small real source corpus, expected course shape, machine/OS constraints, available model credentials, grading expectations for both subjects, and permission boundaries needed to validate the proof of concept against reality.

## Resolution

The Course Author will demonstrate the working product and will not provide private sample decks
or source files. Use small provenance-recorded public fixtures and deterministic fake providers for
repeatable verification; optional live checks may use explicitly free models without exposing
credentials. The development machine provides LibreOffice rather than PowerPoint. These constraints
are maintained in `.scratch/course-harness/verification-context.md`.
